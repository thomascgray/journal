# 01: View a day's photos on the day screen

**Source**: none (user request in conversation, 2026-11-11)

## What and why

The day screen (`src/app/day/[date].tsx`) shows every photo and video the phone took that day
as a 4-column picker. Unattached items are dimmed to 55% opacity, attached ones get a sky-blue
border and a tick, and both stay mixed together in the list. That works for choosing what to
keep, but it is a poor way to look at the day afterwards: the photos the user chose are small
square crops among the ones they rejected, and nothing opens them full size.

This change splits the two jobs. The day screen shows only the day's attached media, laid out
like the journal view, and tapping one opens the existing full-size gallery. Picking moves to
its own screen. In the picker, a tap opens the phone's photo full size and a long press
attaches or detaches it.

## Requirements

Day screen (`/day/[date]`):

1. Below the writing card, the day screen shows the day's attachments in `Masonry`, in
   `attached_at` order, with every attachment shown (no height cap, no "+N" tile).
2. Tapping an attachment opens `/gallery/[date]` with `index` set to that attachment's index.
3. Below the attachments sits a button that pushes `/picker/[date]`. Its label is
   `Add photos` when the day has no attachments and `Edit photos` when it has any.
4. With no attachments, the line `No photos or videos attached.` shows above the button.
5. The day screen does not query the media library or ask for photo permission.
6. On regaining focus, the day screen re-reads the day's attachments, then reads them again once
   any attach or detach writes started for that day have settled.
7. Tags, the writing card, autosave and the day stepper behave as they do now.

Picker screen (`/picker/[date]`, new):

8. Title is `Photos and videos`. Default stack header with a back button.
9. Shows the existing 4-column grid: the phone's media for the day, oldest first, followed by
   attachments no longer on the phone (shown from the app's copy), as `DayEditor` builds
   `items` today.
10. Every tile shows at full opacity. Attached tiles keep the sky-blue border and tick.
11. Tapping a tile opens the full-size viewer at that tile's position in the grid.
12. Long-pressing a tile toggles its attachment, with the same optimistic update and
    `attach`/`detach` calls as today's tap.
13. The status line keeps today's loading, denied, empty and limited-access messages and
    actions, except the ready message, which becomes
    `Tap a photo or video to see it full size. Long-press to attach it to this day.`
14. Screen readers: tiles have role `imagebutton`, `accessibilityState.checked` set to whether
    they are attached, and an accessibility action named `longpress` labelled `Attach` or
    `Detach` that toggles the attachment.

Picker full-size viewer:

15. Opens over the picker on a black background and pages horizontally through every item in
    the picker grid, with the same paging, video and image behaviour as `gallery/[date]`.
16. Top bar, left to right: a close button labelled `✕` (accessibility label `Close`), a title,
    and an attach button.
17. The title is the photo's capture time (as `gallery/[date]` shows it) and `n/total`, joined
    with ` · `, each part omitted when unavailable, `n/total` omitted when there is one item.
18. The attach button reads `Attach` for an unattached item and `Attached ✓` for an attached
    one. Tapping it toggles the current item through the picker's toggle, so the grid behind
    shows the change when the viewer closes.
19. The close button and the Android back gesture close the viewer and return to the picker.

Shared:

20. `gallery/[date]` looks and behaves as it does now after its pager moves into a shared
    component.

## Out of scope

- Captions (`StoredAttachment.caption` exists but nothing edits or shows it).
- Reordering attachments. Order stays `attached_at`.
- Pinch-to-zoom and a "remove from day" action in the gallery viewer.
- Changes to the journal view, apart from `Masonry` gaining an optional prop.
- Haptic feedback on long press.

## Technical notes

Single repo: `C:\Programming\journal`.

Types:

- `StoredAttachment` (`src/db/journal.ts`): `Attachment & { fileName: string | null;
  thumbName: string | null; width: number | null; height: number | null; caption: string }`.
  `Attachment` is `{ assetId: string; mediaType: AttachmentMediaType }`.
- `DayMediaItem` (`src/hooks/useDayMedia.ts`): `{ id: string; mediaType;
  duration: number | null; previewUri?: string | null }`. `id` is a `content://` URI on Android.
- `previewUri(a)` and `fullUri(a)` in `src/db/journal.ts` pick the app's copy or the phone's
  original.

Existing code:

- `src/app/day/[date].tsx`: `DayEditor` renders a `FlatList` of `MediaTile`s with the tags and
  writing card as its header. `useAttachments` (same file) loads once per `dateKey` and toggles
  optimistically through a ref. `MediaStatus` (same file) renders the status line.
- `src/app/gallery/[date].tsx`: horizontal paging `FlatList`, `VideoPage` (expo-video, texture
  view, plays only the current page, at most three players), `useCaptureTime`. The comments on
  `removeClippedSubviews`, `windowSize` and `surfaceType` explain Android bugs; keep them with
  the code they describe.
- `src/app/journal.tsx` `DayCard`: the `Masonry` + `router.push` to the gallery pattern to copy.
- `src/components/Masonry.tsx`: the `MAX_HEIGHT_RATIO` break and the "+N" tile.
- `src/components/MediaTile.tsx`: picker tile; `opacity: attached ? 1 : 0.55`.

Route choice: `src/app/picker/[date].tsx`, a sibling of `gallery/[date].tsx`, rather than a
nested `day/[date]/…` folder next to the `day/[date].tsx` file. It needs no `_layout.tsx` entry.

The picker's viewer is a React Native `Modal` inside the picker screen, not a route, because its
items come from `useDayMedia`, which reads EXIF for every candidate. A route would have to run
that scan again.

Day screen and picker both write and read attachments for the same day. The picker's writes are
async (`attach` inserts the row, then copies the file), so the day screen can regain focus
before they land. Requirement 6 covers this: a module-level record of pending writes per date
in the shared hook.

## Implementation guide

1. **`src/hooks/useAttachments.ts`** (new). Move `useAttachments` out of `day/[date].tsx`.
   Add a module-level `Map<DateKey, Set<Promise<unknown>>>` of in-flight `attach`/`detach`
   calls. `toggle` adds each call's promise and removes it once settled. Export
   `settled(dateKey): Promise<void>`, which resolves after the current set settles
   (`Promise.allSettled`). Keep the toggle's existing optimistic logic.

2. **`src/hooks/useCaptureTime.ts`** (new). Move `useCaptureTime` from `gallery/[date].tsx`,
   changing its argument to `{ id: string; mediaType: AttachmentMediaType } | undefined` so
   both an attachment (`assetId`) and a `DayMediaItem` (`id`) can use it.

3. **`src/components/MediaPager.tsx`** (new). Move the paging `FlatList` and `VideoPage` out of
   `gallery/[date].tsx`. Props:
   `items: PagerItem[]`, `index: number`, `onIndexChange: (index: number) => void`,
   `bottomInset: number`, `emptyText?: string`, where
   `PagerItem = { id: string; mediaType: AttachmentMediaType; fullUri: string;
   previewUri: string | null }`. Keep `getItemLayout`, `removeClippedSubviews={false}`,
   `windowSize={5}`, the ±1 video rule and their comments.

4. **`src/app/gallery/[date].tsx`**. Map attachments to `PagerItem`
   (`id: assetId`, `fullUri(a)`, `previewUri(a)`) and render `MediaPager`. The title logic
   stays here. Check it still behaves the same (requirement 20).

5. **`src/components/Masonry.tsx`**. Add `capped?: boolean` (default `true`). When `false`,
   skip the `MAX_HEIGHT_RATIO` break, so no "+N" appears. Leave the per-tile height clamp.

6. **`src/components/MediaTile.tsx`**. Replace `onPress` with `onPress` (open) and
   `onLongPress` (toggle). Remove the 0.55 opacity. Change the role to `imagebutton`, keep
   `accessibilityState.checked`, and add `accessibilityActions` plus `onAccessibilityAction`
   for `longpress` (requirement 14).

7. **`src/app/picker/[date].tsx`** (new). Parse `date` as `day/[date].tsx` does (same invalid
   date message). Use `useDayMedia`, `useAttachments`, and the `items` merge and `MediaStatus`
   moved from `day/[date].tsx` (requirement 13 sets the new ready text). Hold
   `viewerIndex: number | null`. Tile tap sets it; long press calls `toggle`. Render a `Modal`
   (`visible={viewerIndex !== null}`, `animationType="fade"`, `statusBarTranslucent`,
   `onRequestClose` closes it) containing a black `View`, a `StatusBar style="light"`, a top bar
   padded by `insets.top`, and `MediaPager`. Build `PagerItem`s from `items`: if attached, use
   `fullUri`/`previewUri` of the stored attachment; otherwise `fullUri: item.id` and
   `previewUri: item.mediaType === 'image' ? item.id : null`.

8. **`src/app/day/[date].tsx`**. Swap the `FlatList` for a `ScrollView`
   (`keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"`, same padding). Keep the header content. Remove
   `useDayMedia`, `MediaTile`, `MediaStatus` and the `items` merge. Load attachments with
   `useFocusEffect` from `expo-router`: read `getAttachments`, then `settled(dateKey)`, then read
   again, ignoring results after blur or unmount. Render `Masonry` with
   `width={width - SIDE_PADDING * 2}`, `capped={false}`, and `onPress` pushing
   `{ pathname: '/gallery/[date]', params: { date: dateKey, index } }`. Then the empty line and
   the `Add photos`/`Edit photos` button pushing `{ pathname: '/picker/[date]', params:
   { date: dateKey } }`, styled like the `MediaStatus` action button. Drop the constants and
   imports that are no longer used.

9. Run `npm run typecheck`, `npx expo lint`, `npx expo-doctor` and
   `npx prettier --check "src/**/*.{ts,tsx}"`, then test on a development build
   (`npx expo run:android`).

## Acceptance criteria

1. A day with attachments shows them on the day screen in a two-column masonry at their own
   aspect ratios, with no unattached phone media visible and no "+N" tile, however many there
   are.
2. Tapping an attachment on the day screen opens the black full-size gallery on that item, and
   swiping moves through the day's attachments.
3. A day with no attachments shows `No photos or videos attached.` and an `Add photos` button.
   A day with attachments shows `Edit photos`.
4. Opening a day never shows the photo permission prompt. Opening the picker does, if access has
   not been granted.
5. In the picker, every tile is at full opacity. Tapping a tile opens it full size. Long-pressing
   toggles the tick.
6. In the picker's full-size view, `Attach` attaches the current item and the label changes to
   `Attached ✓`. Closing the view shows the tick on that tile.
7. Attaching or detaching in the picker, then going back, shows the change on the day screen.
   A video attached just before going back appears with its frame once the copy finishes.
8. An attachment whose original was deleted from the phone still shows on the day screen, in
   the gallery, and in the picker, from the app's copy.
9. The journal view's masonry still caps its height and shows "+N".
10. Typecheck, lint, expo-doctor and prettier pass.
